# Reference Verification Report: Thesis_2_revised.docx

Date: 8 October 2026. Citation style: **APA 7th edition** (CSL `apa.csl`, rendered with **citeproc-js 2.4.63**).

## 1. What was done

- Every reference in the bibliography was checked against authoritative sources (Section 3).
- A CSL-JSON record was built for each reference. Authors, volume, issue, pages, year and DOI come from Crossref or DataCite where available. Titles are kept in APA sentence case.
- All in-text citations were replaced with **Mendeley Cite (v3) citation content controls**, which appear as boxed citations in Word. There are **351 citations: 77 parenthetical and 274 narrative**.
  - Each control carries the CSL item data (`MENDELEY_CITATION_v3_…`), so Mendeley Cite can refresh it.
  - Narrative citations keep the author names as running text and use a `suppress-author` citation for the year, for example "Wang (2025)".
- The reference list was regenerated by citeproc-js as one `MENDELEY_BIBLIOGRAPHY` content control. It is APA 7 formatted:
  - journal names, volumes and book titles are in italics;
  - entries have a 0.5 in hanging indent and are left-aligned (the original list was justified and had no italics).

## 5. In-text citation corrections (APA 7)

- Narrative citations of three or more authors are now shortened to "et al.", for example "Hlosta, Zdrahal, and Zendulka (2017)" → "Hlosta et al. (2017)".
- Parenthetical citations use "&". Narrative citations use "and".
- Multiple citations within one set of parentheses are ordered alphabetically.
- First-author initials are added where two first authors share a surname (APA 8.20): **T. Chen and Guestrin (2016)** and **F. Chen and Cui (2020)**.
